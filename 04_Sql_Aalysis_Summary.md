*Tool:* MySQL Workbench

## Key Business Insights

### Revenue Analysis
- Set category = highest revenue at 3.47 Crore
- Set + Kurta = 80% of total revenue
- Saree = premium segment (avg order Rs 806) — underserved!

### Geographic Analysis
- Maharashtra = top state (18,413 orders)
- Bengaluru = top city (10,041 orders)
- Top 5 cities = 29% of all orders

### Time Analysis
- April = peak month (Rs 2.57 Crore)
- May 4 = peak single day (Rs 10.94 Lakhs)
- Revenue declining: April to June (-20.3% total)

### Customer Analysis
- 99.3% B2C orders — pure consumer market
- B2B avg order (Rs 701) higher than B2C (Rs 662)
- B2B = only 0.7% — massive untapped opportunity!

### Size Analysis
- Set and Kurta — M and L most popular
- Saree and Blouse — Free size preferred
